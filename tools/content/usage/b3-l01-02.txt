# Book 3, Lessons 1-2. Format and rules: 00-format.txt

== 3.1
安德思: 安德思的同學 = An Desi's classmates | 安德思是新生。 = An Desi is a new student. | 安德思想轉系。 = An Desi wants to change departments.
開學: 快開學了 = school is about to start | 開學以後 = after school starts | 開學第一天 = the first day of school | 你們學校什麼時候開學？ = When does your school start? | 下星期就要開學了，我還沒準備好。 = School starts next week and I'm not ready yet.
班: 我們班 = our class | 同一班 = the same class | 班上的同學 = the classmates in one's class | 我們班有十二個學生。 = There are twelve students in our class. | 我想換到別的班。 = I'd like to switch to another class.

嚴: 很嚴 = very strict | 管得很嚴 = to keep a tight rein | 對學生很嚴 = strict with the students | 我爸爸對我很嚴。 = My father is strict with me. | 這位教授很嚴，上課不可以遲到。 = This professor is strict; you can't be late for class.
口試: 準備口試 = to prepare for an oral test | 參加口試 = to take an oral test | 口試成績 = the oral test grade | 明天的口試我一點也不擔心。 = I'm not worried about tomorrow's oral test at all. | 口試的時候，老師問了我五個問題。 = In the oral test the teacher asked me five questions.
筆試: 考筆試 = to take a written test | 筆試的題目 = the written test questions | 筆試和口試 = written and oral tests | 筆試比口試容易準備。 = A written test is easier to prepare for than an oral one. | 我筆試考得不錯，可是口試考得很差。 = I did well on the written test but badly on the oral.
以外: 除了他以外 = apart from him | 上課以外的時間 = time outside class | 中文以外的語言 = languages other than Chinese | 除了筆試以外，還有口試。 = Besides the written test, there is an oral test too. | 除了我以外，大家都去了。 = Everyone went except me.
口頭: 口頭報告 = an oral report | 口頭說明 = a verbal explanation | 口頭告訴他 = to tell him verbally | 下星期每個人都要做口頭報告。 = Next week everyone has to give an oral report. | 這只是口頭上的約定，沒有寫下來。 = It's only a verbal agreement; nothing was written down.
報告: 寫報告 = to write a report | 交報告 = to hand in a report | 一份報告 = a report | 這份報告下星期五要交。 = This report is due next Friday. | 我的報告還沒寫完，今天晚上得(děi)熬夜。 = My report isn't finished, so I'll have to stay up tonight.
壓力: 有壓力 = to feel pressure | 壓力很大 = to be under a lot of stress | 工作壓力 = work stress | 考試快到了，我的壓力很大。 = Exams are coming and I'm under a lot of pressure. | 別給自己太大的壓力。 = Don't put too much pressure on yourself.
說明: 看說明 = to read the instructions | 說明書 = an instruction manual | 老師的說明 = the teacher's explanation | 這份說明寫得很清楚。 = These instructions are clearly written. | 請你說明一下為什麼遲到。 = Please explain why you were late.
清楚: 很清楚 = very clear | 說清楚 = to say it clearly | 看不清楚 = can't see clearly | 請你說清楚一點。 = Please say it a little more clearly. | 這件事我不太清楚，你去問老師吧。 = I'm not sure about that; go and ask the teacher.
位子: 找位子 = to look for a seat | 沒有位子 = no seats | 窗戶旁邊的位子 = a seat by the window | 這個位子有人坐嗎？ = Is anyone sitting here? | 教室裡已經沒有位子了。 = There are no seats left in the classroom.
旁聽: 去旁聽 = to go and sit in | 旁聽生 = an auditing student | 旁聽一門課 = to audit a course | 我可以旁聽您的課嗎？ = May I sit in on your class? | 我在旁聽一門法律課，不用考試。 = I'm auditing a law course, so there are no exams.
分: 幾分 = how many points | 一百分 = a hundred points, full marks | 考了九十分 = scored ninety | 你這次考了幾分？ = What did you score this time? | 我只差兩分就考上了。 = I was only two points short of getting in.
羨慕: 很羨慕你 = to really envy you | 羨慕別人 = to envy other people | 讓人羨慕 = enviable | 真羨慕你會說這麼多種語言！ = I really envy you for speaking so many languages! | 不用羨慕別人，你也做得到。 = No need to envy others; you can do it too.
休學: 休學一年 = to take a year off school | 辦休學 = to arrange a leave from school | 休了一年學 = took a year off school | 他休學去工作了。 = He took a break from school to go and work. | 我想休學一年，去國外看看。 = I want to take a year off and see something of other countries.
用功: 很用功 = very hardworking | 用功念書 = to study hard | 用功的學生 = a diligent student | 他每天都很用功，成績當然好。 = He studies hard every day, so of course his grades are good. | 你再不(bú)用功，就要被當(dàng)了。 = If you don't start studying, you're going to fail.
行: 不行 = that won't do | 這樣也行 = that works too | 行不行？ = Is that all right? | 我明天再交，行嗎？ = Is it all right if I hand it in tomorrow? | 只要你喜歡就行了。 = As long as you like it, that's fine.
轉: 轉系 = to change departments | 轉學 = to transfer to another school | 轉到會計系 = to transfer to the accounting department | 我想轉到外語系。 = I want to transfer to the foreign languages department. | 他念了一年就轉學了。 = He transferred to another school after just one year.
原來: 原來的計畫 = the original plan | 原來的工作 = one's previous job | 原來住在台北 = used to live in Taipei | 我原來想念法律，後來選了經濟。 = I originally wanted to study law, but later chose economics. | 原來是你！ = So it was you!
會計: 會計系 = the accounting department | 會計師 = a certified accountant | 公司的會計 = the company's accountant | 我姐姐在一家貿易公司當(dāng)會計。 = My older sister works as an accountant at a trading company. | 念會計的人找工作比較容易。 = People who study accounting find jobs more easily.
熱門: 很熱門 = very popular | 熱門科系 = a popular department | 熱門的話題 = a hot topic | 這幾年，會計系是很熱門的科系。 = Accounting has been a very popular department these past few years. | 這家餐廳非常熱門，要先訂位。 = This restaurant is hugely popular; you have to book ahead.
熬夜: 常常熬夜 = to often stay up late | 熬夜念書 = to stay up studying | 熬了一夜 = stayed up the whole night | 熬夜對身體不好。 = Staying up late is bad for your health. | 我昨天熬夜寫報告，今天好累。 = I stayed up writing a report last night, and I'm so tired today.
當: 被當(dàng)了 = got failed | 怕被當(dàng) = afraid of failing | 當(dàng)了兩門課 = failed two courses | 這門課太難了，我怕會被當(dàng)。 = This course is so hard that I'm afraid I'll fail it. | 那位教授每年都當(dàng)很多學生。 = That professor fails a lot of students every year.
恐怕: 恐怕不行 = I'm afraid that won't work | 恐怕太晚了 = I'm afraid it's too late | 恐怕會下雨 = it will probably rain | 我明天恐怕不能去。 = I'm afraid I can't go tomorrow. | 這麼多功課，今天恐怕寫不完。 = With this much homework, I probably can't finish today.
口才: 口才很好 = to be a good speaker | 練口才 = to practise one's speaking skills | 口才不好 = not good with words | 他口才好，很適合當(dāng)老師。 = He speaks well, so he's well suited to being a teacher. | 我的口才不好，最怕做口頭報告。 = I'm not a good speaker; oral reports are what I dread most.
事: 一件事 = a matter, one thing | 有事 = to have something to do | 沒事 = it's nothing | 你找我有什麼事？ = What did you want to see me about? | 我有一件事想請你幫忙。 = There's something I'd like to ask your help with.
遲到: 別遲到 = don't be late | 上課遲到 = to be late for class | 遲到十分鐘 = ten minutes late | 對不起，我遲到了。 = Sorry I'm late. | 他上班從來不遲到。 = He is never late for work.
差一點: 差一點遲到 = almost late | 差一點忘了 = nearly forgot | 我差一點坐錯車。 = I almost got on the wrong bus. | 我差一點忘了今天有考試。 = I nearly forgot there was a test today.
這樣下去: 再這樣下去 = if this keeps up | 這樣下去不行。 = It can't go on like this. | 再這樣下去，你一定會被當(dàng)。 = If this keeps up, you're sure to fail. | 每天熬夜，這樣下去身體會壞掉。 = Staying up every night, you'll ruin your health if this goes on.
沒辦法: 真的沒辦法 = there's really nothing to be done | 沒辦法去 = unable to go | 我也沒辦法。 = There's nothing I can do either. | 我明天有考試，沒辦法跟你們去。 = I have a test tomorrow, so I can't go with you. | 沒辦法，只好再考一次。 = It can't be helped; I'll just have to take it again.
獨生女: 家裡的獨生女 = the only daughter in the family | 她是獨生女。 = She is an only child. | 我是獨生女，沒有兄弟姐妹。 = I'm an only child; I have no brothers or sisters. | 她是獨生女，父母什麼都給她最好的。 = She's an only child, and her parents give her the best of everything.
私立: 私立大學 = a private university | 私立高中 = a private high school | 私立醫院 = a private hospital | 私立大學的學費比較貴。 = Tuition at private universities is higher. | 他念的是一所私立高中。 = He goes to a private high school.
理想: 很理想 = ideal | 不太理想 = not quite what one hoped for | 理想的工作 = an ideal job | 這次考試的成績不太理想。 = The results of this exam weren't great. | 我的理想是當(dāng)醫生。 = My dream is to be a doctor.
合: 合我的興趣 = to match my interests | 合口味 = to suit one's taste | 不合規定 = against the rules | 這個工作很合我的個性。 = This job suits my personality well. | 這些菜合不合你的口味？ = Are these dishes to your taste?
痛苦: 很痛苦 = miserable, in great pain | 覺得痛苦 = to feel miserable | 痛苦的事 = a painful thing | 念沒興趣的科系很痛苦。 = Studying in a department you have no interest in is miserable. | 每天早上六點起床，真痛苦！ = Getting up at six every morning is torture!
科系: 選科系 = to choose a department | 什麼科系 = which department | 理想的科系 = the department one hopes for | 你念什麼科系？ = What's your major? | 選科系的時候，興趣最重要。 = When you choose a department, interest matters most.
放棄: 不要放棄 = don't give up | 放棄機會 = to give up a chance | 放棄考試 = to give up on the exam | 再試一次，別放棄！ = Try once more, don't give up! | 他為了工作，放棄了念研究所的機會。 = For the sake of his job, he gave up the chance to go to graduate school.
不管: 不管怎麼樣 = no matter what | 不管多忙 = however busy | 不管天氣好不好，我都要去。 = I'm going whether the weather is good or not. | 不管多累，他每天都運動一個小時。 = However tired he is, he exercises for an hour every day. | 不管你說什麼，我都不會放棄。 = Whatever you say, I won't give up.
反對: 我不反對 = I have no objection | 沒有人反對 = nobody objects | 反對這個計畫 = to oppose this plan | 我父母反對我休學。 = My parents are against my taking time off school. | 你為什麼反對這件事？ = Why are you against this?
個性: 個性很好 = a nice personality | 有個性 = to have character | 個性不合 = personalities that clash | 她個性很好，朋友很多。 = She has a lovely personality and lots of friends. | 他們兩個人的個性很不一樣。 = The two of them have very different personalities.
活潑: 很活潑 = very lively | 個性活潑 = a lively personality | 活潑的孩子 = a lively child | 她妹妹又活潑又可愛。 = Her little sister is lively and cute. | 這個班的學生都很活潑，上課很熱鬧。 = The students in this class are all lively, so lessons are animated.
外語: 學外語 = to learn a foreign language | 外語系 = the foreign languages department | 第二外語 = a second foreign language | 他會說三種外語。 = He can speak three foreign languages. | 學外語要多聽多說。 = To learn a foreign language you have to listen and speak a lot.
擔心: 別擔心 = don't worry | 很擔心 = very worried | 讓父母擔心 = to make one's parents worry | 我擔心明天的口試考不好。 = I'm worried I'll do badly on tomorrow's oral test. | 你不用擔心，一定沒問題。 = You needn't worry; it will be fine.
填: 填表 = to fill out a form | 填資料 = to fill in one's details | 填錯了 = filled it in wrong | 請在這裡填你的姓名和電話。 = Please fill in your name and phone number here. | 這張表我填錯了，可以再給我一張嗎？ = I filled this form in wrong; could I have another one?
表: 一張表 = a form | 申請表 = an application form | 交表 = to hand in a form | 請先填這張表。 = Please fill out this form first. | 申請表可以在網路上下載。 = The application form can be downloaded online.
辦: 辦手續 = to go through the procedures | 辦護照 = to get a passport | 怎麼辦？ = What should I do? | 這件事很難辦。 = This is hard to deal with. | 我下午要去銀行辦事。 = I have to go to the bank this afternoon to take care of something.
手續: 辦手續 = to go through the procedures | 休學手續 = the procedures for taking leave from school | 手續費 = a handling fee | 轉系的手續很麻煩。 = The procedure for changing departments is a hassle. | 手續都辦好了，下星期就可以上課。 = The paperwork is all done; I can start class next week.
申請: 申請表 = an application form | 申請學校 = to apply to schools | 申請獎學金 = to apply for a scholarship | 我想申請美國的研究所。 = I want to apply to graduate schools in the United States. | 他申請到獎學金了。 = He got the scholarship he applied for.
成績單: 一張成績單 = a transcript | 交成績單 = to hand in a transcript | 英文成績單 = a transcript in English | 申請研究所需要大學的成績單。 = Applying to graduate school requires a university transcript. | 成績單下星期才會出來。 = The report cards won't be out until next week.
考上: 考上大學 = to get into university | 沒考上 = didn't get in | 考得上 = able to get in | 他考上了理想的大學。 = He got into the university he was hoping for. | 我妹妹今年考上了醫學系。 = My younger sister got into medical school this year.
推薦信: 寫推薦信 = to write a letter of recommendation | 需要推薦信 = to need a letter of recommendation | 老師的推薦信 = a teacher's recommendation letter | 我想請王教授幫我寫推薦信。 = I'd like to ask Professor Wang to write me a recommendation. | 申請獎學金需要兩位老師的推薦信。 = The scholarship application needs letters from two teachers.

== 3.2

摸: 摸一摸 = to have a feel | 摸摸看 = to feel it and see | 不要摸 = don't touch | 這件毛衣摸起來很舒服。 = This sweater feels lovely to the touch. | 請不要用手摸這些畫。 = Please don't touch these paintings.

羊毛: 羊毛衣 = a wool sweater | 羊毛外套 = a wool coat | 羊毛做的 = made of wool | 這件毛衣是羊毛做的。 = This sweater is made of wool. | 羊毛的衣服不能用熱水洗。 = Wool clothes can't be washed in hot water.

打折: 打八折 = twenty percent off | 打對折 = half price | 不打折 = no discount | 這件外套現在打七折。 = This jacket is thirty percent off right now. | 新商品不打折。 = New items aren't discounted.
