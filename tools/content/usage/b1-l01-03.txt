還是: 茶還是咖啡 = tea or coffee | 今天還是明天 = today or tomorrow | 你要喝茶還是咖啡？ = Would you like tea or coffee? | 他是你哥哥還是你弟弟？ = Is he your older brother or your younger brother?
吧: 好吧。 = All right then. | 去吧！ = Go ahead! | 喝茶吧！ = Have some tea! | 我們一起去吧！ = Let's go together! | 我們吃越南菜吧！ = Let's have Vietnamese food!
可以: 可以去 = can go | 不可以 = cannot, not allowed | 可不可以 = can or cannot | 我可以看你的照片嗎？ = May I look at your photos? | 你可以來我家吃晚飯。 = You could come to my place for dinner.
學: 學中文 = to study Chinese | 學游泳 = to learn to swim | 學做菜 = to learn to cook | 我想學打網球。 = I want to learn to play tennis. | 我妹妹想去日本學音樂。 = My younger sister wants to go to Japan to study music.
中文: 學中文 = to study Chinese | 中文書 = a Chinese book | 中文老師 = a Chinese teacher | 他的中文很好。 = His Chinese is very good. | 我覺得中文很好聽。 = I think Chinese sounds lovely.
一起: 一起去 = to go together | 一起吃飯 = to eat together | 一起看電影 = to watch a movie together | 我常和哥哥一起打籃球。 = I often play basketball with my older brother. | 你要不要和我們一起去？ = Do you want to come with us?
吃: 吃飯 = to eat, to have a meal | 好吃 = tasty | 吃晚飯 = to eat dinner | 你想吃什麼？ = What do you want to eat? | 這個菜很好吃。 = This dish is delicious.
晚飯: 吃晚飯 = to eat dinner | 做晚飯 = to make dinner | 一起吃晚飯 = to have dinner together | 今天晚上誰做晚飯？ = Who is making dinner tonight? | 我媽媽做的晚飯很好吃。 = The dinners my mom makes are delicious.
菜: 做菜 = to cook | 越南菜 = Vietnamese food | 日本菜 = Japanese food | 好吃的菜 = tasty food | 你想吃哪國菜？ = Which country's food do you feel like? | 我爸爸很喜歡做菜。 = My dad loves to cook.
越南: 去越南 = to go to Vietnam | 越南人 = a Vietnamese person | 越南菜 = Vietnamese food | 陳小姐是越南人。 = Miss Chen is Vietnamese. | 越南咖啡很好喝。 = Vietnamese coffee tastes great. | 我想去越南玩。 = I'd like to visit Vietnam.
好不好: 好不好？ = OK? | 你來我家，好不好？ = Come over to my place, OK? | 明天去游泳，好不好？ = How about going swimming tomorrow? | 今天晚上吃日本菜，好不好？ = How about Japanese food tonight?
