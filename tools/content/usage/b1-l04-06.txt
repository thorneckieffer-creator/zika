上課: 去上課 = to go to class | 上中文課 = to have Chinese class | 今天不上課 = no class today | 你明天上不上課？ = Do you have class tomorrow? | 我們在五樓上課。 = We have class on the fifth floor.
花蓮: 去花蓮玩 = to take a trip to Hualien | 花蓮的風景 = Hualien's scenery | 花蓮的海很美。 = The sea at Hualien is beautiful. | 我朋友家在花蓮。 = My friend's home is in Hualien.
聽說: 我聽說 = I hear that | 聽說那裡的風景很美。 = I hear the scenery there is beautiful. | 聽說你會做飯。 = I hear you can cook. | 我聽說他去日本了。 = I heard he's gone to Japan.
近: 很近 = very close | 不近 = not close | 很近的地方 = somewhere very close | 那家餐廳近不近？ = Is that restaurant close? | 圖書館很近，在宿舍旁邊。 = The library is close; it's next to the dorm.
方便: 很方便 = very convenient | 不方便 = inconvenient | 不太方便 = not very convenient | 上網買東西真方便。 = Shopping online is really convenient. | 宿舍附近有很多商店，很方便。 = There are lots of shops near the dorm; it's very convenient.
這裡: 在這裡 = here | 來這裡 = to come here | 這裡的學生 = the students here | 這裡可以上網嗎？ = Can I get online here? | 這裡的東西有一點貴。 = Things here are a little expensive.
學生: 一個學生 = a student | 新學生 = a new student | 臺灣學生 = a Taiwanese student | 很多學生 = lots of students | 我是學生。 = I'm a student. | 這個學校有五千個學生。 = This school has five thousand students.
在: 在家吃飯 = to eat at home | 在學校上課 = to have class at school | 在圖書館看書 = to read in the library | 我在宿舍看電影。 = I watch movies in the dorm. | 你想在哪裡吃飯？ = Where do you want to eat?
東西: 買東西 = to buy things, to shop | 吃東西 = to eat something | 很多東西 = lots of things | 好吃的東西 = something tasty | 這是什麼東西？ = What is this? | 這家商店的東西很便宜。 = The things in this shop are cheap.
外面: 在外面 = outside | 學校外面 = outside the school | 外面的餐廳 = restaurants outside | 外面很熱。 = It's hot outside. | 我今天想在外面吃飯。 = I feel like eating out today.
裡面: 在裡面 = inside | 學校裡面 = inside the school | 裡面有什麼 = what's inside | 請進，老師在裡面。 = Please come in, the teacher is inside. | 圖書館裡面有咖啡店。 = There's a coffee shop inside the library.
商店: 一家商店 = a shop | 附近的商店 = the shops nearby | 去商店買東西 = to go to the shop to buy things | 宿舍旁邊有兩家商店。 = There are two shops next to the dorm. | 商店裡面有很多東西。 = There are lots of things in the shop.
吃飯: 去吃飯 = to go and eat | 一起吃飯 = to eat together | 吃晚飯 = to have dinner | 你吃飯了嗎？ = Have you eaten? | 我請你吃飯吧。 = Let me treat you to a meal. | 我們去哪裡吃飯？ = Where shall we go to eat?
宿舍: 在宿舍 = in the dorm | 學生宿舍 = student dormitory | 學校宿舍 = school dormitory | 你的宿舍貴不貴？ = Is your dorm expensive? | 我們宿舍裡面有餐廳。 = There's a cafeteria inside our dorm.
樓: 一樓 = the first floor | 五樓 = the fifth floor | 幾樓 = which floor | 樓上 = upstairs | 教室在幾樓？ = Which floor is the classroom on? | 我的宿舍在三樓。 = My dorm room is on the third floor.
棟: 一棟大樓 = a building | 這棟宿舍 = this dorm building | 哪一棟 = which building | 圖書館是哪一棟？ = Which building is the library? | 那棟房子很舊。 = That house is very old.
大樓: 一棟大樓 = a building | 新大樓 = a new building | 大樓裡面 = inside the building | 這棟大樓很新。 = This building is new. | 我家在那棟大樓的五樓。 = My home is on the fifth floor of that building.
圖書館: 去圖書館 = to go to the library | 在圖書館看書 = to read in the library | 學校的圖書館 = the school library | 我常去圖書館看書。 = I often go to the library to read. | 我們學校的圖書館很大。 = Our school's library is big.
旁邊: 在旁邊 = at the side, next door | 學校旁邊 = next to the school | 旁邊的人 = the person next to you | 坐在我旁邊 = to sit next to me | 我家旁邊有一家書店。 = There's a bookshop next to my home. | 他坐在老師旁邊。 = He sits next to the teacher.
教室: 在教室 = in the classroom | 去教室 = to go to the classroom | 我們的教室 = our classroom | 教室裡面沒有人。 = There's nobody in the classroom. | 我們的教室在二樓。 = Our classroom is on the second floor.
游泳池: 去游泳池 = to go to the pool | 學校的游泳池 = the school pool | 在游泳池游泳 = to swim in the pool | 我們學校有游泳池。 = Our school has a swimming pool. | 這個游泳池很大。 = This pool is big.
