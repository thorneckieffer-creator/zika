看病: 去看病 = to go see a doctor | 去醫院看病 = to go to the hospital to see a doctor | 我下午要去看病。 = I'm going to see a doctor this afternoon. | 在臺灣看病很方便。 = Seeing a doctor in Taiwan is very convenient.

跟: 跟老師說 = to tell the teacher | 跟他說話 = to talk to him | 我有話要跟你說。 = I have something to tell you. | 你跟醫生說了嗎？ = Did you tell the doctor? | 他跟我說他生病了。 = He told me he was sick.
油: 很油 = very greasy | 有一點油 = a bit greasy | 太油的東西 = food that is too greasy | 這個菜太油了。 = This dish is too greasy. | 生病的時候別吃太油的東西。 = Don't eat greasy food when you're sick.

別: 別客氣 = don't be so polite | 別忘了 = don't forget | 別怕 = don't be afraid | 別喝太多咖啡。 = Don't drink too much coffee. | 太晚了，別出去了。 = It's too late, don't go out.
