要是: 要是有空 = if there is free time | 要是太貴，我就不買了。 = If it's too expensive, I won't buy it. | 要是明天天氣好，我們就去旅行。 = If the weather is good tomorrow, we'll go on our trip.
就: 那我就不去了。 = Then I won't go. | 要是你去，我就去。 = If you go, I'll go. | 你喜歡就買吧。 = If you like it, go ahead and buy it. | 要是太遠，我們就坐計程車。 = If it's too far, we'll take a taxi.
貓空: 去貓空喝茶 = to go to Maokong for tea | 貓空的茶館 = the tea houses of Maokong | 貓空的風景很美。 = The scenery at Maokong is beautiful.
