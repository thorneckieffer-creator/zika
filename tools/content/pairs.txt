# The "which pattern?" pairs — 句型, the drill in the Drill tab.
#
# A pair is two to four patterns that both look as if they fit. Each has the one-line test that decides between
# them, a side-by-side line for every choice (the same frame with each choice used correctly), and sentences with
# a blank. The English is shown with the sentence: it is what makes one choice right, so write it to say so.
#
#   == key | 就／才 | 1.11 → 2.11        the key the tallies are kept under (never change one), the pair as shown,
#                                        and Book.Lesson: where the first pattern is taught → where the choice becomes real
#   choices: 就 / 才                     2 to 4, in the order the buttons show. ∅ is "nothing goes here".
#   test: …                              the test that decides it, one line, plain English
#   points: 就=n11-2 才=n18-4            optional: the grammar point each choice belongs to (its tally moves too)
#   side: 就 | 他八點就來了。 | He came at eight: early.
#   中文 with ___ | answer | English | why        a sentence; add  | a/b  to offer other choices than the pair's
#   pick: right sentence | wrong | wrong || English || why      for word order: choose the whole sentence
#
# A sentence is asked only from the lesson by which all its characters have been met; tools/check_pairs.py works
# that out, checks the rest, and installs the file:   python3 tools/check_pairs.py --write
# Read the list through after a change:              python3 tools/check_pairs.py --list
#
# The first eighteen pairs and their tests are Thorne's, from his teacher-checked notes (21 November 2026). The
# last six, and the older sentences of six of the first eighteen, were the "Which one?" drill until v65.

== zai | 到／在 | 1.5 → 2.6
choices: 到 / 在
test: Moving verbs take 到, placing verbs take 在. 坐、拿、走、放 take both and the meaning flips.
points: 到=n21-1 在=n21-3
side: 到 | 我把書搬到樓上。 | I moved the books upstairs: they travel there.
side: 在 | 我把書放在樓上。 | I put the books upstairs: that is where they stay.
那張椅子，請你放___樓下。 | 在 | That chair, please put it downstairs. | Placing verb, stays there → 在.
我把球踢___學校外面了。 | 到 | I kicked the ball outside the school. | Movement, arrives somewhere → 到.
這些字，我要寫___本子上。 | 在 | These characters, I’ll write them in my notebook. | 寫在 — they stay on the page.
他想把公司搬___台南。 | 到 | He wants to move the company to Tainan. | 搬到 — moving to a destination.
我正___網站上找租房子的資料。 | 在 | I’m looking for rental info online right now. | 正在 + V = in progress.
我們從台北開___台南。 | 到 | We drove from Taipei to Tainan. | 從…V到… — arriving at the end point.
錢存___銀行比較安全。 | 在 | Money is safer kept in the bank. | 存在 — placed and stays.
他每天坐公車___學校。 | 到 | He takes the bus to school every day. | 坐…到 — the ride ends there.
你的名字請寫___這裡。 | 在 | Please write your name here. | 寫在 — it stays on the page.
請你把車開___我家前面。 | 到 | Please drive the car to the front of my house. | 開到 — moving to a point.
老師坐___我旁邊。 | 在 | The teacher is sitting next to me. | 坐在 — where she ends up sitting.

== jiucai | 就／才 | 1.11 → 2.11
choices: 就 / 才
test: 就 = sooner or more than expected, 才 = later or less. 才 also = "only if". 才 refuses a final 了.
points: 就=n11-2 才=n18-4
side: 就 | 他八點就來了。 | He came at eight: earlier than I expected.
side: 才 | 他八點才來。 | He didn't come until eight: later than I expected.
如玉五歲___會寫字了。 | 就 | Ruyu could already write at five. | Earlier than expected → 就.
安同七歲___會寫字。 | 才 | Antong couldn’t write until he was seven. | Later than expected → 才.
你看！我買的這件衣服___兩百塊。 | 才 | Look! This shirt I bought was only 200. | 才 + amount = merely.
我以為垃圾車六點來，沒想到五點半___來了。 | 就 | I thought the truck came at six; it turned out it came at 5:30. | Sooner than expected → 就 … 了.
他每天很晚___睡覺，所以上課很累。 | 才 | He doesn’t sleep until very late, so he’s tired in class. | Late → 才.
我一到家___打電話給你。 | 就 | I’ll call you as soon as I get home. | 一…就… = as soon as.
電影八點開始，他九點___到。 | 才 | The film started at eight; he didn’t arrive until nine. | Later than expected → 才.
下個星期___得準備考試了。 | 就 | Next week we already have to prepare for the exam. | Sooner than you’d like → 就.
他十點___來。 | 才 | He didn't come until ten. | Later than expected, and no 了 at the end → 才.
你做完功課___可以出去玩。 | 才 | You may go out to play only once your homework is done. | "Only if" → 才.

== xianzai | 再／然後 | 1.12 → 2.11
choices: 再 / 然後
test: Can a new subject follow? Yes → 然後. No → 再.
points: 再=n12-1 然後=n19-5
side: 然後 | 先吃飯，然後我們去看電影。 | First we eat, and then we go to a film: 我們 follows 然後.
side: 再 | 先吃飯，我們再去看電影。 | First we eat, then we go to a film: 再 comes after 我們, before the verb.

我們先吃飯，我___送你回家。 | 再 | Let's eat first, then I'll take you home. | It comes after the subject 我 → 再.
你先往前走，___你會看到一家超商。 | 然後 | Go straight first, and then you'll see a convenience store. | 你 follows it → 然後.
你先休息一下，我們___出發。 | 再 | You rest a bit first, then we'll set off. | After the subject 我們 → 再.
他先打電話給我，___我們一起去了車站。 | 然後 | He called me first, and then we went to the station together. | 我們 follows it → 然後.
你先把功課寫完，我們___去公園玩。 | 再 | Finish your homework first, then we'll go and play in the park. | After the subject 我們 → 再.
老師先說了一次，___學生跟著說。 | 然後 | The teacher said it once first, and then the students repeated it. | A new subject (學生) follows → 然後.
你先想一想，明天___告訴我。 | 再 | Think it over first, and tell me tomorrow. | Before the verb, no subject after it → 再.
我們先坐捷運，___我朋友開車來接我們。 | 然後 | We take the MRT first, and then my friend comes to pick us up by car. | 我朋友 follows it → 然後.
