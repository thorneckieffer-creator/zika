Before the deck moves, so that the deck that moves is whole. **The check before it is applied** (his, 21 November), answered on 22 November from `js/sync.js` as it is in v66:
  - *What an import does.* It replaces the Mac's deck, marks the Mac as holding unsent work and sends the whole deck to the room. The room keeps one deck, the last one sent.
  - *A device that was closed, with nothing unsent, and has synced before*: on opening it takes the room's deck without asking. This is the good case.
  - *A device that was closed with older unsent changes*: on opening it stops and asks, "Use theirs" or "Keep mine". **Keep mine sends its older deck to the room, and every other device that is open with nothing unsent takes it at once: the import is undone everywhere.** Use theirs takes the merged deck and drops whatever that device had not sent.
  - *A device that is open with unsent changes at the moment the import arrives* does not ask. It shows a note for seven seconds ("the Mac also saved; this device wins", with a button to take the Mac's instead) and then sends its own deck over the import.
  - *So the clash is not fixed. It is only less likely.* The room sync still swaps whole decks, and a device with unsent work can still put an older deck over a newer one, by a wrong tap in the first case and by doing nothing in the second. What v63 changed is narrower: an empty deck can no longer be sent over a full one, and a page that has not finished starting cannot write at all.
